## The fields that matter

**Name.** What a shopper searches for and what appears on their order. Write it the way a customer
would say it, not the way your supplier's invoice does.

**Price.** In your store's currency.

**Images.** At least one. A product with no image sells badly, and the listing has a gap where
every other product has a picture.

**Category.** How the product is found by browsing rather than searching. A product can be in more
than one.

**Stock.** How many you have. See **Stock and availability** for what happens when it reaches zero.

## The fields that can wait

Descriptions, SEO fields, badges and related products all improve a product page and none of them
stops you from selling. Get the catalogue in, then improve it.

## Before you publish a product

Look at it on the shop, not only in the form. The product page is where the two parts of your work
meet — the catalogue and the theme — and a description that reads well in a textarea can be a wall
of text on a phone.
