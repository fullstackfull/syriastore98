## When to use a variant

Use variants when the thing being sold is the same product in different forms: a shirt in three
sizes, a soap in two scents. Use separate products when a shopper would think of them as different
things.

The test is how somebody would ask for it. "Do you have that in blue?" is a variant. "Do you have
the other one?" is a separate product.

## Price and stock per variant

Each variant can carry its own price and its own stock. A large size that costs more is a price on
the variant, not a second product. Stock is counted per variant, so selling the last medium does
not make the shirt unavailable.

## Keep the set small

Every option you add multiplies the number of variants: three sizes and four colours is twelve
rows to price, stock and photograph. Add the second option only when you really sell every
combination.
