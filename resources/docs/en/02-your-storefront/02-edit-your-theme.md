## Where the editor is

In the dashboard, under the theme section. It shows your active theme's pages, and each page's
sections, and each section's fields.

## What you can change

Text, images, colours, and whether a section appears at all. The fields you see are the ones your
theme actually declares, so they differ between themes — a theme with a testimonials section has
testimonial fields and a theme without one does not.

## Nothing you type is live yet

This is the part worth internalising. Changes you make are saved as a **draft**. A draft:

- is visible to you and to nobody else;
- does not change what a customer sees;
- can be previewed exactly as the shop would render it;
- stays until you publish it or replace it.

So you can work on a redesign across several days, on a shop that is open and taking orders, and
nothing leaks out until you decide.

## Permissions

Saving and previewing a draft needs access to the store. **Publishing** needs more: the store's
owner, a colleague holding the theme permission, or the platform operator. That separation is
deliberate — preparing a change and putting it in front of customers are different decisions.

## If two people edit at once

The editor notices if the live version changed since you started, and refuses to publish over
somebody else's work rather than silently overwriting it. Re-open the editor, look at what changed,
and publish again.
