## What a theme is

A theme decides how your shop looks: its layout, its typography, how products are arranged on a
listing, what the home page is made of. It does not hold your products, your orders or your
customers — those belong to the store and survive any change of theme.

Which themes you may use is set by your plan.

## Light and dark

Every theme renders in a light and a dark presentation, and a visitor's own preference is
respected. You do not choose one; you get both. Colour choices you make in the editor are checked
against both so text stays readable either way.

## Arabic and English

A theme lays itself out right to left in Arabic and left to right in English, from the same
settings. You do not maintain two designs.

## Changing theme later

You can. What carries over and what does not is worth knowing before you do it:

- **Products, orders, customers, coupons:** untouched.
- **Theme settings** — the text, images and colours you set in the editor — belong to the theme
  you set them on. Switching to another theme shows that theme's own defaults; switching back
  shows your settings again.

So changing theme to "see what it looks like" is safe, but do it from a draft and preview it
rather than publishing it over a live shop.
