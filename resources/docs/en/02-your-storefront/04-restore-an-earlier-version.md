## The history

Every publication is recorded. The editor shows them newest first, with who published and when,
and offers to restore any of them.

Your store's first entry is captured the first time you open the editor or save a draft, so even a
shop that has never been edited has something to go back to.

## Restoring

Restoring publishes an old version as the new live one. It is an ordinary publication: the history
grows, it does not rewind. You can restore the restore if you change your mind.

## What going back does not undo

A restore **writes back** the settings that version held. It does not delete settings created
afterwards, because deleting is not what "go back" means to most people and quietly removing a
setting somebody added last week would be worse than leaving it.

In practice: if you added a banner last Tuesday and restore a version from Monday, the banner is
still there. The editor lists the settings in that situation so you can clear them deliberately.

## When to use it rather than editing

Use a restore when a published change was wrong and you want the previous state **now**. Use the
draft and preview when you are making a considered change. Restoring is a repair, not a workflow.
