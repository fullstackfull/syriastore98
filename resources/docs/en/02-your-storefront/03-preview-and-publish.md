## Preview

From the editor, open the preview. It renders your shop through the real storefront — the same
theme, the same layout, the same code a customer gets — with your draft applied.

The preview is private. It is not indexed by search engines, it is not cached, and the draft never
leaks into an ordinary visitor's view of your shop.

Look at it in both languages if you sell in both, and on a phone as well as a computer. Most of
what goes wrong in a redesign goes wrong on a narrow screen.

## Publish

Publishing applies the whole draft at once. There is no moment in which half of your change is
live: either the previous version is showing or the new one is.

After publishing, the draft becomes the live version and a new entry appears in your publication
history.

## One thing publishing does not do

Publishing **adds and changes** settings. It does not delete ones that are not in the draft. If a
setting existed before and your draft says nothing about it, it stays as it was.

That matters most when restoring an old version: a setting that did not exist back then is not
removed by going back. The editor tells you which settings those are rather than leaving you to
discover them on the shop.
