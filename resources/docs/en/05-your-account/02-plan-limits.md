## Where to see them

The pricing page lists every plan with its limits exactly as the platform applies them, and your
dashboard shows what you are currently using.

## Reaching one

A limit refuses the action that would exceed it. The product beyond your product limit is not
created; the store beyond your store limit is not opened. Nothing already there is removed or
hidden.

## Reading the three answers

A plan's limits are shown in three forms, and they mean different things:

- a **number** is the ceiling;
- **Unlimited** means there is no ceiling;
- **not specified** means that setting was never configured on that plan — ask rather than assume.

Storage is in megabytes and counts the images and files you have uploaded.

## Before you change plan

Check what you are using against what the new plan allows. Moving to a smaller plan while you are
over its limits is a problem to resolve first, not afterwards.
