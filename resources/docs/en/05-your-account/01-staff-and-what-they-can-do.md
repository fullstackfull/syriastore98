## Give people their own sign-in

Sharing the owner account is tempting and it costs you three things: you cannot tell who did what,
you cannot remove one person's access without changing everybody's password, and you cannot limit
what any of them can reach.

How many staff accounts you may create is set by your plan.

## Roles

A role is a named set of permissions. Create the roles your shop actually has — somebody who packs
orders, somebody who maintains the catalogue — and give each one only what that job needs.

Two permissions deserve particular thought:

- **Publishing a theme.** This changes what every customer sees, immediately. Preparing a draft is
  a separate, lower permission on purpose, so a colleague can work on the shop's appearance without
  being able to put it live.
- **Anything touching prices, orders or customer data.** Give it to the people whose job it is.

## Removing access

When somebody leaves, remove their account. Changing the shared password is not a substitute,
because there should not be a shared password.
