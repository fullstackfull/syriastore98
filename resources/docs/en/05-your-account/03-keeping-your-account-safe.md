## A password that is only yours

Not the one from your email, not the one from your supplier portal. If any of those is ever
exposed, a reused password hands over your shop with it.

## A second step at sign-in

Two-factor sign-in is available depending on your plan. It is the single change that makes a
stolen password survivable, and it costs you a few seconds a day.

## One account per person

Covered in **Staff and what they can do**, and repeated here because it is a security measure and
not only a tidiness one: shared accounts cannot be audited and cannot be revoked individually.

## When somebody leaves

Remove the account the same day. Not at the end of the month, not when you next think of it.

## If something looks wrong

An order you did not expect, a product you did not change, a sign-in from somewhere you have never
been — tell us before you tell anyone else, and give us enough detail to see what you saw.
