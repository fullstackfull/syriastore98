## The store is where everything lives

Products, orders, customers, coupons, the theme — all of it belongs to a store, not to your
account. If you open a second store later it starts empty; nothing is shared between them except
your sign-in.

## What you set when you create one

**Name.** What customers see. You can change it.

**Web address.** Derived from the name. This is how people reach your shop, so changing it later
breaks every link anyone has saved or shared. Think about it once now rather than twice later.

If the address you want is already taken by another merchant, or is a word the platform reserves
for its own pages, you are given the next one that is free — for example `my-shop-1` instead of
`my-shop`. Nothing is lost; you can still rename the store.

**Language.** Arabic or English. This is the language your shop opens in; visitors can switch.

**Currency.** What prices are shown and charged in.

## Next

Pick a theme and make it yours before you add products — it is easier to judge a catalogue inside
