## What you need

An email address you control. Everything the platform sends you — the confirmation, password
resets, notices about your subscription — goes there, so use an address you will still have in a
year, not one belonging to somebody who happens to be helping you today.

## Register

1. Open the sign-in page and choose to register as a merchant.
2. Enter your name, your email address and a password.
3. Give your first store a name. You can change the display name later; the web address derived
   from it is harder to change, so pick something you will still want to be known by.
4. Submit the form.

## Confirm your email address

The platform sends a confirmation message. Until you open the link in it, parts of the dashboard
stay closed to you — that is deliberate: it proves the address works before anything important is
sent to it.

If the message has not arrived after a few minutes, check the spam folder before asking for another
one. Repeated requests do not make it arrive faster.

## After that

Your account is the owner of everything you create. Staff get their own sign-ins later, with the
access you decide — see **Staff and what they can do**. Do not share the owner account.
