## What a plan decides

A plan is not only a price. It sets, for your whole account:

- how many **stores** you may open;
- how many **products** you may have;
- how many **staff accounts** you may create;
- how much **storage** your images and files may use, in megabytes;
- whether you may use a **custom domain** or a **subdomain**;
- whether **shipping methods** and **tax rules** are available to you;
- whether your store can be offered as an **installable app**;
- which **storefront themes** you may use.

## How to read the limits

On the pricing page each plan lists its limits exactly as the platform applies them.

- A **number** is the ceiling. If products say `500`, the five hundred and first is refused.
- **Unlimited** means the plan sets no ceiling at all.
- **Not specified** means that setting was never configured on this plan. It is not the same as
  "no" — ask before you subscribe if it matters to you.

## Changing your mind later

You can move to a different plan. What you cannot do is keep more than the new plan allows: if you
drop to a plan with fewer stores than you have open, sort that out before you switch, not after.
