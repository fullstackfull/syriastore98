## Payment

Which payment methods you can offer depends on the providers configured for your installation and
on your plan. Set them up before you take the shop live, and place one real order yourself through
each method you enable.

That test order is not optional ceremony. A payment method that is configured but wrong fails at
the last step of the customer's purchase, which is both the most expensive place to fail and the
one you are least likely to hear about — most people simply leave.

## Delivery

Shipping methods are available depending on your plan. Set what you charge, where you deliver, and
how long it takes, and describe them the way you would to somebody standing in front of you.

Where you do not deliver matters as much as where you do. A customer who completes an order you
cannot fulfil costs you a refund and a reputation.

## Tax

Tax rules are available depending on your plan. If you charge tax, set it up before your first
order rather than correcting invoices afterwards.
