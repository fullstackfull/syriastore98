## 4. The one Flutter finding that was measured but unapplied — and it was half right

`docs/design/GOLDEN-BASELINE-FRAMING.md` (previous account) measured the golden baselines as a card
stretched to 800px and proposed a one-line harness fix, deliberately unapplied because Flutter was
not installed.

**Every measurement in it reproduces. Its proposed cause does not.** `Align` alone changed nothing
— the recaptured images were still 98.3% / 98.2% one flat colour with the last differing row at
`y = 799`. The other half is in the widgets: a `Column` with no `mainAxisSize` is
`MainAxisSize.max`, so it fills any bounded height.

```
card            in a scrolling list (what the app draws)   in a bounded 800px box
customer        141.0px                                    800.0px
delivery man    120.0px                                    800.0px
transaction     128.0px                                    800.0px
coupon          140.0px                                    800.0px
top order       168.0px                                    800.0px
```

All five are repaired and `test/ui/card_surface_census_test.dart` is table-driven, so the next card
is a row in a map rather than another investigation. It also asserts the palette fact the cards kept
rediscovering, once: `LightThemeColor.cBackGround`, `AppColor.cWhite` and `Colors.white` are all
`0xFFFFFFFF`, so a light card has no surface distinction to inherit.

**One lead, deliberately not claimed.** `settingCard` built with the app's own
`DefaultImages.statisticIcn` throws a RenderFlex `hasSize` assertion during `performLayout` in this
harness. That is either a genuine layout fragility in the card or a context the harness does not
supply, and the two are not distinguishable without reading it properly. It is out of the census
with the reason recorded, not quietly dropped — adding it once understood is one line.

After both halves, `customer-card.dark-ltr.png` reads **82.2% page / 15.8% card** with the last
differing row at **`y = 142`**, instead of 98.2% card and `y = 799`.

**And it uncovered a defect that document had retracted.** It withdrew the claim that the card's
fill equals the page's; the retraction was right about dark and wrong about light.
`LightThemeColor.cBackGround` is `0xffFFFFFF` and the light cards' fill is `Colors.white` — the
same colour, separated by a shadow at **1.08:1**. Invisible while the card covered the page. All
three cards now carry a 1px `LightThemeColor.cBorder` hairline in light (1.43:1) and no outline in
dark, where `#18263F` on `#0F1A2E` already reads.

**The eight regenerated images are NOT committed.** `test/golden/goldens/*.png` is gitignored by
owner instruction — "candidates, not approved baselines" — and route 1 step 2, a human looking at
every image in both themes and both directions, is unchanged.
