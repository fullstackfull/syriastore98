# Master Plan — project-wide gap register

Every recovered requirement that is **not** VERIFIED COMPLETE. Each gap keeps a stable ID
and traces back to its requirement row in `MASTER-PLAN-REQUIREMENT-TRACEABILITY.md`.

> **A CLOSED GAP KEEPS ITS ROW.** The document's practice has always been to retain a resolved
> gap with its closure note rather than delete it, so the history of what was wrong and what fixed
> it stays attached to the ID. Such a row carries `VERIFIED COMPLETE`. The sentence above
> describes what gets a row in the first place, not what stays.

> **THE REGISTER HAS TWO TABLE SHAPES AND ONLY ONE OF THEM HAS A STATUS COLUMN.** The original
> **181** rows are `| gap | requirement | phase | status | what remains | next action |`. The
> **70** rows added since — the 18 recovered on 2026-11-16 (`GAP-182`…`GAP-199`), the 31 raised
> by later work (`GAP-200`…`GAP-230`), 2 more untracked requirements found on 2026-11-24
> (`GAP-231`…`GAP-232`), **5 raised on 2026-11-25 by the independent review of the GAP-131 fix**
> (`GAP-233`…`GAP-237`), **2 more the same day from the 13-agent census of the storefront API**
> (`GAP-238`…`GAP-239`), **2 found while FIXING those** (`GAP-240`…`GAP-241`), **4 raised the
> same day by the independent review of the GAP-233/234/235 fix** (`GAP-242`…`GAP-245`), **1
> raised while closing the last of the GAP-142 families and WITHDRAWN the same day as false**
> (`GAP-246`), and **2 carried out of the same batch's reviews** (`GAP-247`…`GAP-248`) — are
> `| gap | decision id(s) | phase | measured | next action |`: they record what was MEASURED in
> this container, and carry no status term. **251 rows in total.** The status counts below
> therefore describe the 181, and say nothing about the other 70. That is a real limitation, not a
> rounding: progress against the later rows cannot be counted mechanically, only read.

> **TWO OF THE FOUR (`GAP-242`, `GAP-243`) WERE FIXED IN THE SAME CHANGE THAT RAISED THEM** and
> carry `VERIFIED COMPLETE` in their text. Recorded as rows anyway rather than fixed silently: both
> were live, reachable, destructive defects that the suite did not cover, and the pattern that found
> them — reviewing a fix and then sweeping the codebase for the SAME STATEMENT elsewhere — is the
> thing worth keeping, not just the two patches.

> **A category-A gap must never be silently downgraded to C.** If one moves, the move needs
> an owner decision recorded against the gap ID.

> **Category assignment is mine, not the owner's.** It is derived from each row's status,
> gap text and next action — stated so nobody reads it as an owner classification.
