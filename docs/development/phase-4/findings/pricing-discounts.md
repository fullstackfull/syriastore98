Part of `../PHASE-04-FINDINGS-REGISTER.md`. Inventory by one agent; every claim below then

## What this domain is

Price resolution has two engines that do not agree. `Product::ProductPrice()` (app/Models/Product.php:316) resolves a line price from the catalogue plus the store's FlashSale and Tax rows, and that is what the cart loops over; `Coupon` discounting then happens TWICE on every checkout, once per cart ROW inside `cart_list`/`cart_list_cookie` via `Cart::getCouponTotalAmount()` and once over the whole subtotal inside `ApiController::apply_coupon()`. The gated engine is `apply_coupon` — P1-ORD-01 gave it a server-derived spend baseline, a fail-closed refusal and an unsupported-type refusal, all well tested — but the ungated per-row engine is the one that produces `total_sub_price`, and `total_sub_price` is what `PaymentService::getProductStatus()` writes into `orders.final_price` for `cod` and `bank_transfer`, while `apply_coupon`'s own returned `final_price` is OVERWRITTEN at ApiController.php:5332 with that same per-row arithmetic. The single most important thing to know: the per-row engine checks only expiry and the two usage limits — it never looks at `minimum_spend`, `maximum_spend`, or `coupons.status`, and it subtracts a `flat` coupon's whole amount from every cart row. Every coupon-authorization test in the suite drives `POST /api/{slug}/apply-coupon` with a single-row basket, so the divergence is invisible to it. Separately, `coupons.status` is written by all four coupon editors and read by nothing in the codebase, and the Flutter merchant API's coupon create/update/delete endpoints carry no permission check at all while the sibling `couponlist` read does.
